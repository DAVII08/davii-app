Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Summary: This App is a personal tool. It does not collect, sell, or share any personal data from any user. It only uploads video files to the Operator's own TikTok account.

1. Who We Are

DAVII Music Uploader is a personal application operated by DAVII, an independent music artist based in Seoul, South Korea. Contact: lsuy0109@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

This App collects no personal data from any user. The App is used exclusively by its Operator to automate video uploads to TikTok. There are no sign-ups, no user accounts, and no data entry by third parties.

The only data processed by the App is:

  • An OAuth access token issued by TikTok to the Operator's own account, stored locally on the Operator's personal device only.
  • Video files created by the Operator, used solely for upload purposes.

3. TikTok API Data

The App uses the TikTok Content Posting API with the following scopes:

  • video.upload — to upload video files to the Operator's TikTok account.
  • video.publish — to publish uploaded videos on the Operator's TikTok account.

No data retrieved from TikTok is stored, shared, or used for any purpose other than confirming successful upload.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any data with any third party. No analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking services are used.

5. Data Retention

The OAuth access token is stored locally on the Operator's device and deleted when no longer needed. No server-side storage exists.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13. No data from children is knowingly collected.

7. Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected from any individual other than the Operator, there are no data subject requests to process. If you believe your data has been affected, contact us at lsuy0109@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact
